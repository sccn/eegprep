## 2025-05-15 - [runica Training Loop Optimization]
**Learning:** Moving `np.errstate` context managers outside of tight iterative loops and replacing internal helper wrappers (like `_matmul`) with native operators (like `@`) significantly reduces function call overhead and repeated context entry/exit cycles. In `runica`, where loops can run for hundreds of thousands of iterations, these micro-optimizations compound into measurable gains. Additionally, replacing explicit identity matrix multiplications and broadcasting with `onesrow` with direct NumPy broadcasting further streamlines the computational path.
**Action:** Always check for repeated context manager entries or redundant function wrappers in high-iteration loops and prefer direct NumPy broadcasting over manual matrix construction for bias/mean adjustments.
